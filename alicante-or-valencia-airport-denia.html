Alicante or Valencia? Choosing your airport for Denia

DPC
Denia Property Care
10 September 2026 · 5 min read

Denia sits between two international airports, which is one of the nicer accidents of geography on this stretch of coast. Neither is a bad choice — but they're not identical, and the difference matters more when you're arriving late or carrying golf clubs.

Alicante (ALC) — usually the answer

Alicante handles the most flights and the most low-cost carriers, and it's the airport most visitors to Denia use. It's also the shorter transfer, and the AP-7 motorway route is now toll-free.

  • Road distance: roughly 104–105 km.
  • Typical private transfer time: about 1 hour 10 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes, depending on traffic and exactly where in Denia you're going.
  • Longer journey times quoted by some operators usually reflect shared shuttles or intermediate stops.

Valencia (VLC) — worth comparing

  • Road distance: roughly 117 km.
  • Typical transfer time: about 1 hour 20 minutes.

If the flight times or fares work better, Valencia is a perfectly comfortable transfer rather than a compromise. If you're price-comparing, compare the total: two cheap seats from the wrong airport can cost more than two pricier ones from the right one.

What to check before booking any transfer

  • Is the flight number tracked, so a delay doesn't mean a missing driver?
  • Does the price include meet & greet inside arrivals, or a car park meeting point?
  • Is it a fixed price per vehicle, or per person?
  • Do you need child seats, or extra room for golf bags, bikes or a pushchair?
  • Who do you call if the flight is diverted?
A transfer is only as good as the ten minutes after a delayed flight lands.
